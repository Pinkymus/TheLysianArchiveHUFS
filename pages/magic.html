Aura

The unseen force that shapes Auropia.

Aura (Aura) is an energy possessed by living creatures in Auropia. A person's ability to use aura depends on both their natural capacity and their learned control.

It flows through the body, connects to the world around it, and forms much of the Lysian society; from healing and craftmansship to power and politics.

Aura and Biology

The ability to develop aura is an inherited biological trait, but it remains dormant unless a person is exposed to enough ambient aura.

Aura users also release aura into their surroundings. Over time, this creates a feedback loop where aura-rich environments produce more aura users, who then make the environment even richer in aura.

Brands

A brand is an engraving placed directly upon a person's aura. The visible scar left on the body is only a physical consequence of the deeper mark.

Brands are powered by the branded person's own aura. Because of this, suppressing the flow of aura can weaken a brand's influence, although doing so for too long can be dangerous.

Aura Stones

Aura stones are gemstones whose crystal structures allow them to carry and interact with aura.

They can be used in their natural form or engraved with runes that guide the way aura flows through them. Stones with regular, well-ordered crystal structures are generally the most suitable.
